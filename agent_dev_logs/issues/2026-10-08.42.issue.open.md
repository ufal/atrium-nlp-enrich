# [\#42 Issue](https://github.com/ufal/atrium-nlp-enrich/issues/42) `open`: TEITOK from a born-digital record: page size and line boxes from pages[].canvas and lines[].bbox
**Labels**: `enhancement`, `development`, `integration`


#### <img src="https://avatars.githubusercontent.com/u/65828620?u=80b39d3cb2967c52e1d4af28dcefc7a1efdfdc79&v=4" width="50">[K4TEL](https://github.com/K4TEL) opened issue at [2026-10-08 12:19](https://github.com/ufal/atrium-nlp-enrich/issues/42):

A born-digital record from digital-convert (`POST /reformat`) carries a canvas per page and a box per line. `POST /enrich` with such a record and no ALTO answers `layout_source: "rows"`: one `<pb>` per page, one `<lb>` per line, no boxes and no `<facsimile>`, so TEITOK cannot show the text over its page. This is W5 of ufal/atrium-digital-convert#4.

**Wanted:** where a page has `pages[].canvas` and its lines `lines[].bbox`, write them as the TEITOK layout (a `<surface>` per page sized by the canvas, the line box on each `<lb>`) and answer `layout_source: "record"` (additive in the spec). Pages without them keep the rows layout.

**To decide:**
- word boxes: the record has none; leave them out, or apportion the line box by character offset?
- units and origin of `canvas.unit` and `bbox` against what the writer expects (`api_util/bbox_scale.py` already rescales ALTO);
- page images: the record has none.

**Done when** a PDF record from `/reformat` goes through `/enrich` to a TEITOK with `<surface>` and boxed `<lb>`s that passes `tests/test_teitok_conformance.py`, tested on a fixture from digital-convert's samples.
