## 0. Governing principles

Everything below is subordinate to three rulings. When a task here conflicts with one of them, the ruling wins.

**P1 — Apply, don't reorganise.** AMCR and TEATER remain authoritative in how their terms are organised and interpreted. Nothing in this workstream constitutes a change to either source vocabulary. Any actual change to AMCR needs a dedicated decision process outside this issue.

**P2 — Facets are plumbing.** They organise and prioritise the prompt. They are not semantic output and not a new taxonomy. Keep "which terms are available to the model" strictly separate from "how those terms are grouped for inference" — in the code, the config, and the output schema alike.

**P3 — Keep by default; exclude on evidence.** A TEATER branch comes out only when enrichment results show it is irrelevant or introduces systematic, otherwise unsolvable noise. Purely technical/administrative AMCR lists are the standing exception.

## 2. Workstream A — Vocabulary scope

### A2–A5 — ✅ done and in force

Seven technical AMCR lists excluded with a stated reason each (240 terms); `udalost_typ` kept and moved to `Location & Admin`; `Documentation` removed from the config **and** absent from the built vocabulary; `muzeum` (TEATER 220) and `památková péče` (253) moved via `teater_branch_map`'s most-specific-first resolution. Artifacts rebuilt and committed; `--from-flat --check` exits 0.

### A1 — Reinstate the excluded branches (P3) — ⛔ **blocked on @motyc, nothing changed**

The decision package is `data_samples/vocab/6.O3O4.decision-package.md`, backed by `exclusion_impact.csv`. It splits one intimidating question into two answerable ones:

**Q1 — geographic, ethnic and dynastic terms, 982 terms.** TEATER 2560 *etnika* (391), 2900 *historické oblasti* (317), 3076 *dynastie* (15), AMCR `zeme` (250) and `jazyk` (9). These are the **only** excluded terms that collide with the standing rule at `llm_run.py:171` — *"NEVER select a country name, language name, or geographic region name"*. Three outcomes, one of which is a trap:

1. keep excluded → guardrail and config stay consistent; nothing to do;
2. reinstate **and** relax the guardrail wording in the same change → coherent, and measurable;
3. reinstate and leave the guardrail → **avoid.** The scores would measure the contradiction rather than the model.

**Q2 — everything else, 1708 terms.** TEATER 288 *Hraniční obory*, 3094 *Povolání*, 3549 *Společnost*, 1 *Teorie*, plus 2558 battles and 3091 wars. No guardrail implication whatsoever, so it can be answered entirely on its own and is much the easier of the two. Worth stating plainly when asking: under **P3** the default is *keep*, and these were excluded on an a priori reading rather than on evidence from results — so the burden sits the other way round.

**Our job here is to make either answer cheap, not to pre-empt it.** Every branch is a one-line `teater_branch_map` switch; say so, so the decision doesn't feel expensive. **Do not** reinstate anything and **do not** touch the guardrail wording until Q1 is answered: today the two are consistent, and changing either alone breaks that.

## 3. Workstream B — Dedup and term identity — ✅ **done**

`to_term_pairs()` groups same-label records instead of dropping the losers; the survivor carries the rest as `discarded_ids` (M7). Exclusion runs **before** dedup, which is what recovers `papír`, `úřední písemnost`, `olej` and `vodní pramen` — each pinned by a regression test naming the exact ids. `taxonomy_overrides.json` keys corrections on `(source, id)`, never on a bare label, and is checked ahead of both maps (O5, closed).

### B3 — Bracketed qualifiers — ✅ mechanism done, ⛔ review outstanding

M8 is built, and deliberately not finished. A same-label group is **one concept by default**; a record splits into its own bracketed entry only when an override says so. Auto-splitting on a differing English gloss was tested against the data and rejected — it would have split `přírodní útvar` (*natural landform* / *natural feature*) and `cesta` (*roads* / *path/road* / *path*), which are translation variance. Issuing the verdicts is @david-spacil's under M10.

**What we did to make that review tractable.** "139 rows to read" reads as an afternoon; it is not, because most rows are noise. `collision_review.csv` ranks the **127** differing-gloss groups by gloss dissimilarity, so the likely homonyms come first — validated against the one confirmed case, `zámek`, which lands **10th of 127**. The least-similar rows carry everything interesting (`barvivo` *colourant*/*dye*, `tesák` *bowie knive*/*dussack*, `hamr` *forge*/*hammer mill*, `žlab` *ditch*/*gutter*, `příkop` *ditch*/*moat*, `kůl` *pole*/*stake*); the high scorers are self-evidently one concept (`amfora`: *amphora*/*amphorae*). The other **343** collisions share a gloss and need no reading at all.

**Hand-off shape:** ~30 rows to read, ~97 to bulk-confirm. Each verdict is one `qualifier_cs` entry in `taxonomy_overrides.json` — no code change per verdict. We supply the sheet; we do not supply the verdicts.

### D1 — The rubric *(draft it, don't decide it alone)*

Write it as an issue comment for @david-spacil and @motyc to ratify. It must answer, in writing, **before** any annotation begins:

- Are form labels (`OBEC`, `Druh nálezu`) meta-text?
- Is a less specific but correct term (`hrob` when `pohřeb kostrový` exists) wrong, or partially credited?
- Are `extracted_keywords_cs/en` scored separately from `teater_category`, or not at all?
- **Does `teater_category_ids` participate in scoring, or only the label?** (new, from B2 — an id set can be right when the label is ambiguous)
- **Either side of a composite pair counts correct** (from §4 — the rule `same_as` exists to support)
- How `Nerelevantní (meta-text)` is scored, and whether a false meta-text is worse than a wrong category

`annotation/GUIDELINES.md` is the structural template: its per-label boundary rules, "General rules" section and explicit nesting policy map one-to-one onto these questions.

### D3 — Paradata provenance *(small, precise, and a licensing gap too)*

- `para_config.txt:44-45` declares `amcr_vocab` and `teater_data` as **conditional CC BY-NC 4.0** components, and `llm_run.py` calls `log_component` **zero** times. A run's paradata therefore records neither the licence obligation nor which vocabulary produced the output. Follow the pattern already used in `keywords.py`.
- `data_samples/vocab/union_nested.meta.json` **already carries exactly what D3 needs** — `taxonomy_config{path,sha256}`, `taxonomy_overrides{path,sha256}`, `counts`, and the TEATER `snapshot_ref` — and nothing reads it. Load it in `llm_run.main()` and fold its identity into the logger's `config` block. This is what makes "do these outputs come from the same build?" answerable by looking.

### D4 — The harness *(port it, don't write it)*

Most of it already exists in `atrium-llm-enrich`, built for a different benchmark (hub #22, OCR/VLM transcription), and `pandas` is already a runtime dependency here:

| Reuse from llm-enrich | For |
|---|---|
| `eval_metrics.normalize_text()` | NFC + casefold + whitespace collapse before any label comparison |
| `eval_metrics.entity_prf()` | micro P/R/F1 over multisets of `(type, text)` — the shape of `extracted_keywords_*` scoring |
| `eval_metrics.score_corpus()` | per-tier + overall macro aggregation; re-key tiers as facets or models |
| `sample_stratify.py` | the sampler, already solved — reads `--lines-dir data_samples/DOC_LINE_CATEG` in the exact column shape this pipeline emits, buckets pages into quality tiers, samples deterministically (`seed=42`), writes a manifest with splits |

Genuinely new is only the **category-specific scoring**: exact match on `teater_category`, partial credit for a correct-but-less-specific term, composite-pair equivalence via `same_as`, id-set scoring if D1 says so, and the meta-text confusion cell. A small module on top of ported primitives — not a harness from scratch.
