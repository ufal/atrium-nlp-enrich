## 🔎 Verified state — 2026-09-14

Every citation below was re-checked with `grep -n` / `sed -n` against nlp-enrich `bfabfca` and hub `f675943`.

### The constraint is already stated in two of the three required places

**1. The manifest carries it.** Hub `docs/templates/k8s/atrium-service.deployment.yaml:21-23`:

```yaml
  replicas: 1                        # raise once a repo's job/state model is verified
                                      # safe under >1 replica — see the nlp-enrich note
                                      # in docs/k8s_deployment.md §"Known limits".
```

This is not a uniform policy that implies all five services scale alike. It is the **safe** default, made explicit, with an inline pointer to the per-service exception.

**2. `docs/k8s_deployment.md` states it in prose, twice**, under `## Known limits — read before promising ARÚP/ARÚB more than this delivers` (`:212`):

- `:226-232` — *"**nlp-enrich's job queue is in-memory** (`service/jobs.py`) … a job's **record** does not survive a restart … do not represent this manifest as making the job API safe to poll across a restart."*
- `:233-238` — *"**`replicas: 1` is the template default**, not a recommendation to stay at one. Raising it is safe for the four request/response services (translator, page-classification, alto-postprocess, llm-enrich) … nlp-enrich's in-memory job store means a client's poll can land on a *different* replica than the one running its job, which reads as 404 rather than 'still running'."*

That bullet names the four services that may raise it, names the one that may not, and gives the reason. It is the design work the previous edition of this digest called "the actual design work in the issue", already done.

**Both predate the issue.** `git show 193697e:docs/templates/k8s/atrium-service.deployment.yaml` contains the `replicas: 1` stanza with its comment, and `git show 193697e:docs/k8s_deployment.md` carries the `replicas: 1` bullet at its then-line `:108-113`. So: present **at or before** the hub mirror root commit `193697e`, dated 2026-09-08 12:08 — two days before #35 was opened.

**Acceptance bullet 1 is satisfied.** The issue's own wording is *"Either `docs/k8s_deployment.md` states nlp-enrich's replica constraint **and** the manifest carries it, **or** a `POST /jobs` to one replica is retrievable by `GET /jobs/{id}` from another."* The first branch holds on both halves.

### The code, unchanged — and correctly so

- **Process-local registry.** `service/jobs.py:19-20`:

  ```python
  _jobs: Dict[str, Job] = {}
  _job_lock = asyncio.Lock()
  ```

  `create_job()` at `:23` inserts under that lock, in one uvicorn process's address space. `git log -- service/jobs.py` returns **exactly one commit**, the nlp mirror root `643457e` (2026-09-04 11:14) — the file is untouched across the whole visible history.
- **Process-local workspace.** `service/enrichment.py:42` (**drifted** from the `:39` cited by the issue and the previous edition):

  ```python
  _API_JOBS_ROOT = Path(os.environ.get("API_JOBS_ROOT", _REPO_ROOT / "TEMP" / "api_jobs"))
  ```

  Used at `:372` (mkdir), `:444` (healthcheck workspace) and `:467` (per-job workspace). `_KEEP_WORKSPACES` is at `:44` (drifted from `:41`), and gates the `shutil.rmtree` at `:451-452`, `:563-564` and `:683-685`.
- **The consequence still holds.** `GET /jobs/{id}` (`service/api.py:520-525`) reads `_jobs.get(job_id)` and 404s on a miss, so it resolves only on the replica that accepted the `POST` (`@app.post("/jobs")` at `:476`). Under `replicas: 2` behind a Service, a client polling its own job gets a 404 roughly half the time, for a job running correctly on the other pod. What has changed since the previous edition is **not** this behaviour — it is that the deployment docs now warn about it.
- **Factor VIII is otherwise correct.** One uvicorn process per container; **no uvicorn `--workers` flag in any of the five repos** (the only `--workers` in the ecosystem is nlp-enrich's batch CLI at `keywords.py:678`, defaulting to `DEFAULT_WORKERS` = CPU count at `:86`, which is process-pool parallelism inside one container and env-tunable — not an HTTP-server worker count). In-flight job concurrency is bounded by `MAX_CONCURRENT_JOBS` (`service/api.py:42`) via the semaphore at `:53`.

## ✏️ Corrections to this digest's own previous edition

The 2026-09-10 edition measured this repo accurately and never opened the hub files it was describing. Six of its statements are now wrong, and four of those were wrong on the day they were written.

1. **Status line** — *"This repo is the ecosystem's only stateful service, and the manifest we hand ARÚP/ARÚB does not say so."* The second half was false when written. The manifest said so at `atrium-service.deployment.yaml:21-23`, at or before `193697e` (2026-09-08 12:08), two days before the issue opened.
2. **§"Why this is a handover problem, not just a design note"** — *"It carries one replica policy for all five services. So the document that tells ARÚP/ARÚB how to deploy this service implies it scales like the other four."* The one policy **is** a single replica — the safe default — carrying an inline comment that points a reader at the nlp-enrich note in `docs/k8s_deployment.md` §"Known limits". It implies the opposite of what this paragraph claims: it implies **nobody** scales until their job/state model has been verified.
3. **§Open, first bullet** — *"it has to be *stated*, and stated in three places (manifest, `k8s_deployment.md`, acceptance runbook), or it is not a resolution."* The three-place bar was the right bar. **Two of the three are done.** Only the runbook is outstanding, and the sentence as written reads as though none of it is.
4. **§Open, second bullet** — *"A uniform manifest with a per-tool exception needs a mechanism … Nobody has said which, and this is the actual design work in the issue."* Somebody had. The mechanism chosen is "default every service to one replica, name in prose which four may raise it and why the fifth may not" — manifest `:21-23` plus `docs/k8s_deployment.md:233-238`.
5. **§Verified state** — *"`service/enrichment.py:39`"* and *"`service/enrichment.py:41` reads `API_KEEP_WORKSPACES`"*. Drifted to `:42` and `:44`. Likewise the per-job log cited as `service/enrichment.py:234` is now at `:254` (`"LOG_FILE": f'"{ws}/out/processing.log"'`).
6. **§Open, fourth bullet (#61)** — *"a per-job log on container-local disk is only meaningful if jobs are pinned to a pod … Decide `/jobs`'s promise here; let #61 follow."* #61 did not wait, and did not need to: its D3 relay landed on 2026-09-12 at `service/enrichment.py:306-348` and changed nothing about workspace lifetime. The prediction that the two were separable held; the ordering claim did not.
